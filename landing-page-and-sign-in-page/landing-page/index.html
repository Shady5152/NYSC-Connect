FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NYSC Connect free to use?

Yes! Creating an account, joining LGA communities, and browsing basic guides are completely free for all corps members.

How are accommodation listings verified?

Every listing is checked by our local field agents before it goes live on the platform.

Can I find a roommate on the platform?

Yes, our roommate matching tool connects you with fellow corps members posted to your area.

Is my data secure?

We use industry-standard encryption to keep your personal and deployment information safe.

How do I join my LGA chat group?

Once you complete your profile with your LGA, you're automatically added to the relevant group.

Can I list my apartment as a landlord?

Yes, landlords can register and list verified properties through our dedicated landlord portal.
